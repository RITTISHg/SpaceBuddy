NASA's open NASA's central public API portal, providing free programmatic access to datasets 
including the Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD), Mars Rover Photos, and the Space Weather Database (DONKI). 
A free API key (higher rate limits than the shared demo key) can be requested instantly and is a good starting point for any challenge team building a data-connected prototype.
